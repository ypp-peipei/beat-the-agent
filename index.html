A 90-second game about reinforcement learning

Beat the Agent

You run the batteries on a neighborhood feeder with a lot of rooftop solar, for one day.

  • 1You play. At noon, solar pushes the voltage too high. In the evening, EV charging pulls it too low and power is expensive. Tap Charge, Pause or Discharge at the right time.
  • 2An agent learns. It starts by pressing buttons at random and practises 40,000 days, right here in your browser.
  • 3Compare. Nobody tells the agent the right move. Reward is its only teacher.

Score = dollars saved compared with doing nothing. Each 15 minutes outside the voltage limits costs a penalty.

What is real here

The feeder is a six-bus radial line solved by power flow at every step. The agent is tabular Q-learning. It trains on random days in your browser and is then tested on the day you played. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The feeder, prices and penalties are illustrative, not a real utility's.
